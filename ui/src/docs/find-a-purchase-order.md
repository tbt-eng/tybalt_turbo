# Find and print a purchase order

These lists are under **Purchase Orders**. Every person can open them.

## Fix a rejected PO

**Rejected (Mine)** lists your POs that an approver rejected. The red label shows who rejected the PO, and why.

1. Open **Rejected (Mine)**.
2. Select **Edit and Resubmit**.
3. Correct the PO.
4. Select **Save**.

Turbo sends the PO for approval again. You can delete a rejected PO that has no PO number.

## Search for a PO

1. Open **Search**.
2. Type two or more characters of the PO number, vendor, job, client, description or owner.
3. Select **Active**, **Closed** or **Cancelled**.

Search doesn't list a PO before its approval. Only the owner, the approvers, the managers of the job and staff who can view reports see a Closed or Cancelled PO.

## Stale POs

**Stale** lists each Active PO that got its approval more than 30 days ago. A PO in normal use is on the list too. Turbo doesn't close a stale PO.

Tick **Expense only** to list the POs that a person pays for themselves. **Provisional Remaining** is the total less all expenses, also the ones that aren't committed.

## Recurring POs that end soon

**Expiring (Recurring)** lists each Active Recurring PO that ends in the next 30 days or is past its end date. To look further, change the number of days and select **Load**.

Turbo refuses an expense with a date after the end date. You can't extend a Recurring PO. Create a new PO.

## Print a PO

You can print an Active PO.

1. Open the PO.
2. Select **Print**.
3. Check **Amount to print**, or enter a lower amount.
4. Tick **Plus tax** or **Plus shipping** if the vendor adds them.
5. Select **Proceed**.

The printed PO shows only the amount that you chose.

## Amounts on a printed PO

For a Cumulative PO, Turbo offers the total less the committed expenses. If you tick **Plus tax** or **Plus shipping**, the extra cost must fit in the rest of the approved amount. A higher cost needs a new approved PO.

## See who can approve POs

PO approvers and staff who can view reports see **PO Approvers** under **Business**. It lists each approver's limit for each kind of PO, and their divisions.

Select a division to see who can approve for it. **Not configured** means that an admin has not set the person's limits. Select **Help** on that screen to see how a limit applies.
