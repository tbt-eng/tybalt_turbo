# Review and track time

These lists are under **Time Management**. Your menu shows a list only if you can use it.

## See the time sheets you approved

Managers see **Approved By Me**. It lists every time sheet that you approved, with its hours. A committed time sheet shows **Committed**.

Select the week-ending date to open a time sheet. To take back an approval, select **Reject** there. You can't reject a committed time sheet.

## See time sheets shared with you

**Shared with Me** lists the time sheets that a manager shared with you. Select **Details** to open one. You can read it. You can't approve or reject it.

## Track the time sheets of a week

Staff who can view reports see **Tracking**.

1. Open **Tracking**.
2. Select a week-ending date.
3. Select **Sheets**, **Missing**, **Ignored** or **Not Expected**.

**Sheets** groups the submitted time sheets as Approved, Submitted and Committed. A committer selects **Commit** or **Reject** on an approved time sheet.

## Find missing time sheets

**Missing** lists the people who must submit a time sheet and have none for the week. A recalled time sheet counts as none.

Select **Email** to write to a person. A committer selects **Ignore This Week** to move a person to **Ignored**. **Expect This Week** moves the person back.

## Download the time of a week

Select **Weekly Time** or **By Staff** beside a week on **Tracking**. Turbo downloads a CSV file. **Weekly Time** holds committed time only.

## Find a work record

Staff who can view reports, and staff who check work records, see **Work Records**.

1. Open **Work Records**.
2. Select **K**, **Q** or **F**.
3. Type two or more characters of the number.
4. Select a number.

Turbo shows each time entry with that number. The number comes from the **Work Record** field of a time entry.
