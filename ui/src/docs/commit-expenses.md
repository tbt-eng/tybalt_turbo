# Review and commit expenses

These lists are under **Expenses**. Your menu shows a list only if you can use it.

## See the expenses you approved

Managers see **Approved By Me**. It lists the expenses that you approved, newest first. Select **Load More** for older expenses. Search finds only the expenses on the screen.

To take back an approval, open the expense and select **Reject**. You can't reject a committed expense.

## Settle a foreign-currency expense

Payables staff see **Settlement**. It lists each approved On Account or Corporate Credit Card expense in a foreign currency. A committer can't commit it before you settle it.

1. Open **Settlement**.
2. Select **View** to check the receipt.
3. Enter the amount paid in **Settled CAD Total**.
4. Select **Save**.

Turbo accepts an amount within 20% of the CAD equivalent on the screen.

## Correct a settlement

1. Select **Settled**.
2. Select **Clear** on the expense.
3. Select **Unsettled**.
4. Enter the right amount.
5. Select **Save**.

You can do this until a committer commits the expense. If someone rejects the expense, Turbo removes the settlement.

## Commit an expense

Committers see **Commit Queue**. It lists each approved expense that is ready.

1. Open **Commit Queue**.
2. Select **Attachment** to check the receipt.
3. Select **Commit**, or select **Reject** and enter a reason.

Turbo commits the expense at once. A commit is final.

## If Turbo refuses a commit

- The PO of the expense is no longer Active.
- The expenses of a Cumulative PO go over its total by more than Turbo allows. See [Purchase orders](/docs/purchase-orders#spend-more-than-the-po-total).

A foreign-currency expense that payables staff must settle is not in the queue until they settle it.

## Track committed expenses

Staff who can view reports see **Tracking**. It lists each week that has committed expenses. Turbo puts an expense in the week of its commit, not the week of its date. A week ends on Saturday.

Select a week-ending date to see its expenses for each person.

## Download the expenses of a week

Select **Expenses** beside a week on **Tracking** to download a CSV file. Select **Receipts** to download the attachments.

**Incomplete** means that the CSV file leaves out a person who has a temporary payroll ID.
