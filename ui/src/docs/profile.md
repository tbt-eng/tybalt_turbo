# Profile

Your profile holds your name, your manager and your defaults. You can change it at any time.

## Open your profile

1. Open the menu.
2. Select your email address under **Account**.

## Change your profile

1. Open your profile.
2. Change the values.
3. Select **Save**.

## Your manager

Your **Manager** approves your time sheets and expenses. Select a person who approves time sheets. Turbo refuses any other person.

## Your defaults

Turbo uses **Default Division**, **Default Role** and **Default Expense Payment Type** on a new time entry or expense. You can change the value on each record.

## What you can't change

An admin sets your default branch and your time off balances. These are on your admin profile. See [Admin profiles](/docs/admin-profiles).
