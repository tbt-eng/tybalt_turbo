# Vendor invoices

A vendor invoice is the bill that a vendor sends for a purchase. Every **On Account** expense needs its invoice details. One invoice can cover several expenses.

## Enter the invoice details

1. Select **On Account** as the payment type.
2. Add the invoice as the attachment.
3. Enter the **Invoice number**, **Invoice date** and **Invoice total**.
4. Select **Save**.

The invoice total is the full total, with taxes. An older **On Account** expense needs these details the next time that you save it.

## What Turbo refuses

- An invoice number that this vendor already has on another invoice. Turbo ignores capitals, spaces and punctuation in this check.
- Expenses that total more than their invoice.
- An expense with a different vendor or currency from its invoice.

## Use one invoice on several expenses

Only a bookkeeper can do this.

1. Open an expense that has the invoice.
2. Select **Create another expense with this attachment**.
3. Select **Create Expense** on the next PO.
4. Enter the total for this expense.
5. Select **Save**.

You can also upload the same file on a new expense. Turbo fills in the invoice details.

## See the expenses on an invoice

Open an expense that has the invoice. **Accounted for** compares the expenses with the invoice total. **Expenses on this invoice** lists each expense.

## Change the invoice details

The person who uploaded the invoice can change the details. A bookkeeper can too. Turbo locks the details when another expense on the invoice goes to approval. A bookkeeper can still fill in an invoice that has no details.
