# Close or cancel a purchase order

The PO's owner or payables staff can close or cancel a PO. You can't undo this. Turbo records who did it and the date.

## Which one to use

- **Cancel:** an Active PO that has no expenses. No PO can use its number again.
- **Close:** a Recurring or Cumulative PO that has a committed expense. No expense can wait for approval or commitment.

Turbo closes a One-Time PO when a committer commits its expense. See [PO types](/docs/purchase-orders#po-types).

## Close or cancel a PO

1. Open the PO. You can also use its row in **My Purchase Orders**.
2. Select **Cancel Purchase Order** or **Close Purchase Order**.
3. Read the warning.
4. Select the same button in the warning to confirm.

After this, no expense can use the PO.
