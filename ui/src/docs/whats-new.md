# What's new

This page is for people who already know Turbo and want a high-level view of recent changes. For details, or to learn how to use Turbo, see the [other Help pages](/docs/start-here).

## October 25 to 31, 2026 (to 5c35b0a0)

- Date fields now start on your device's date. In the evening, forms no longer show tomorrow's date. (v0.3.150)
- The **Stale** PO list has an **Expense only** filter. (v0.3.151)
- You can close a project that has no contact or manager. (v0.3.152)
- You can charge time and costs to a Corporate branch job without Corporate branch access. (v0.3.153)

## October 18 to 24, 2026 (to 9e3446ea)

- **Branch Hours** shows branch managers the hours that staff worked between branches. (v0.3.148)
- After you approve or reject an expense, Turbo opens the next expense that waits for you. (v0.3.147)
- A value change correction can keep its PDF. (v0.3.146)
- Admins can link older subjobs to their project with **Adopt subjobs**. (v0.3.149)
- An expense that exactly fills a PO now saves. (v0.3.144)

## October 11 to 17, 2026 (to d71196b6)

- **My Reimbursements** shows what payroll repaid you on each pay day. (v0.3.124)
- A project's value now changes only with approval. Procurement selects **Propose new value**, and the approver uses **Value Approvals**. (v0.3.125, v0.3.135, v0.3.136)
- Procurement can move a project to another client with **Change Client**. (v0.3.132, v0.3.133, v0.3.134)
- The job details page has a new layout, and **Close project** works on any Active project. (v0.3.139)
- Job details now show a client's alias. (v0.3.125)
- Client details now show the divisions with committed time. (v0.3.141)
- Turbo hides **Delete** for clients and vendors that are in use. (v0.3.130, v0.3.131)
- A PO that Turbo closes now shows when and why. (v0.3.143)

## October 4 to 10, 2026 (to b378bc5a)

- Each client now has one workspace for its details, contacts, invoicing profiles, jobs and notes. (v0.3.118)
- A client can have an alias, and each invoicing profile selects the name to bill. (v0.3.122)
- **Copy to tomorrow** copies an Allowance or Mileage expense. (v0.3.120)
- **Time Off** shows if each person is hourly or salaried. (v0.3.119)
- Turbo now counts Recurring PO payments by calendar date. (v0.3.121)

## September 27 to October 3, 2026 (to 2c738950)

- **WIP** compares your active projects by value used. Branch managers and KPI staff can also see branch and division views. (v0.3.114, v0.3.115)
- **PO Approvers** shows approval limits to PO approvers and staff with report access. (v0.3.112, v0.3.113)
- An Active project now needs a completion date and a value. The completion date can't come before the award date. (v0.3.116)
- You can print a PO with a lower amount, and with tax or shipping as extra. (v0.3.117)

## September 20 to 26, 2026 (to 5eb5bb90)

- Job time summaries now use the job's rate sheet. Turbo uses staff default rates when no job rate matches. (v0.3.109, v0.3.110)
- Each job has a **WIP** tab that compares time, expenses and PO commitments with the project value. (v0.3.111)

## September 13 to 19, 2026 (to af1f7cd8)

- A Recurring PO now closes when a committer commits its last permitted expense. (v0.3.107)
- Payables staff can't close a PO while its submitted expenses wait for approval or commit. (v0.3.108)
- The payroll report now lists the newest periods first. (v0.3.106)

## September 6 to 12, 2026 (to 8d0cb207)

- The **Commit Queue** no longer shows rejected expenses. (v0.3.105)

## August 30 to September 5, 2026 (to c8d6f49f)

- The PO page now shows the approval stage, kind, branch and approvers. (v0.3.100)
- Staff with KPI access can compare hours across branches in **Staff Branch Hours**. (v0.3.101)
- On a PO that needs two approvals, the first approver's limit can be below the total. (v0.3.103)

## August 16 to 22, 2026 (to 0fb6d579)

- You can now enter negative hours on a time amendment. (v0.3.99)
